🔍 WHY THIS HAPPENED:

   Your docker-compose.prod.yml has:
   
   frontend:
     depends_on:
       backend:
         condition: service_healthy
   
   This means:
   1. Backend starts first
   2. Docker waits for backend to be "healthy"
   3. Then frontend starts
   
   When you pressed Ctrl+C, it interrupted step 3!
